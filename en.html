ImmoAnalyzer
Decide in minutes whether a property is really worth it.

Many properties look attractive at first glance – but after interest, service charges, taxes and upkeep, less is often left than expected. Immo Analyzer runs all the key figures for you and reliably shows whether a property stacks up financially and where the risks lie.

  • Rental yield and cashflow at a glance – instead of tedious spreadsheets
  • Closing costs fully included: notary, agent and real-estate transfer tax
  • Flexible financing calculations: classic, fixed monthly payment or full repayment
  • Depreciation and after-tax cashflow, including sale planning
  • Compare properties and get an AI-powered assessment
  • Regional market data and price alerts for new opportunities
